WorkHub: Gmail, Slack, Calendar, Claude, ChatGPT and WhatsApp in a single window.
Requires macOS 12 or later.

INSTALLATION
1. Drag WorkHub onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open WorkHub from the Applications folder.

IF MACOS WON'T OPEN IT ("the developer cannot be verified")
This is normal: the app isn't distributed through Apple, so macOS asks for confirmation the first time.

macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:
  1. Try to open it, then close the warning.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Near the bottom you'll see "WorkHub was blocked": click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password.

macOS 14 or earlier:
  Right-click WorkHub in Applications > Open > Open.

Alternatively, from Terminal:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WorkHub.app

FIRST LAUNCH
- Allow notifications when asked.
- Sign in to the services with your accounts: they are saved only on your Mac.
- WhatsApp: scan the QR code with your phone.
- Shortcuts: Cmd+1...6 for the services, Cmd+T for Todos, Cmd+Shift+F for Focus.
- Focus sounds: add your own audio files from "Add sounds…" in the Focus panel.
